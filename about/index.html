About Arcade Games Hub

Arcade Games Hub (Games Hub) is a completely free online collection of browser games. We are a small team of developers and designers who love classic mini games, and our goal is to bring the games that accompanied generations back to today's players in the lightest, cleanest way possible.

Who we are

Arcade Games Hub (Games Hub) is a completely free online collection of browser games. We are a small team of developers and designers who love classic mini games, and our goal is to bring the games that accompanied generations back to today's players in the lightest, cleanest way possible.

Why we built this site

Many gaming platforms are crowded with pop-up ads, forced sign-ups and bloated client downloads. We believe a good game should be „play on click“ — no installs, no personal data, just open the page and start.

What we offer

Today we host 6 classic mini games: Snake, 2048, Tetris, Minesweeper, Gomoku and Memory Match. All are pure front-end, fast-loading, ad-free and tuned for both mobile and desktop. We keep adding more quality mini games over time.
