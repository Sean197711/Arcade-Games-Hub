🔢 2048

Slide tiles, merge equal numbers, and climb from 2 all the way to 2048!

Score0
Best0

🎯 How to play

  • Desktop: ↑↓←→ or WASD to slide.
  • Mobile: swipe up/down/left/right on the board.
  • Equal tiles merge into their sum when they collide.
  • A new 2 or 4 appears after each move.
  • Reach 2048 to win; no moves left means game over.

💡 Pro tips

  • Keep the largest tile pinned in a corner.
  • Slide along only two directions to stay ordered.
  • Arrange big tiles in a snake pattern for fast merges.
