🗺️ Maze Adventure

Use the arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move the yellow ball through the blue-walled maze to the green exit.

Steps0

🎯 How to play

  • Desktop: ↑↓←→ or WASD to move.
  • Mobile: tap the direction buttons below the game.
  • Blue blocks are walls; the yellow ball is you.
  • Reach the green exit tile to win; fewer steps is better.

💡 Pro tips

  • Survey the whole maze and plan a rough route.
  • Turn back at dead ends instead of forcing through.
  • Remember branching paths to avoid looping.
