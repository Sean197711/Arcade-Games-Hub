⚫ Gomoku

You play Black and move first against the AI — connect five stones in any direction to win!

Game statusYour turn (Black)

🎯 How to play

  • Click an intersection to place your stone; you play Black and move first.
  • First to connect five stones in any direction — row, column or diagonal — wins.
  • The built-in AI plays White with a balance of attack and defense.
  • A full board with no winner is a draw.

💡 Pro tips

  • Open near the center to control the board.
  • Build open threes and fours to force the pace.
  • Watch for the AI's threes and block them in time.
