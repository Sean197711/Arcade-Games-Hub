💣 Minesweeper

Numbers show how many mines sit in the 8 surrounding cells. Flag them all to win!

Mines left10
Status🙂
Time (s)0

🎯 How to play

  • Left click (tap) to reveal a cell.
  • Right click (long press) to flag a mine, again to cancel.
  • A number tells how many mines are around it.
  • Reveal all non-mine cells to win; first click is always safe.

💡 Pro tips

  • Start from corners and edges for clearer info.
  • When enough flags surround a number, other cells are safe.
  • Mark unsure cells and break elsewhere first.
