💣 Minesweeper

The number tells you how many mines hide in the 8 surrounding cells. Deduce and flag them all!

Mines left10
Status🙂
Time (s)0

🎯 How to play

  • Left-click (tap on mobile) to reveal a cell.
  • Right-click (long-press on mobile) to place or remove a flag.
  • A number shows how many mines are in the 8 surrounding cells.
  • Reveal all safe cells to win; your first click is always safe.

💡 Pro tips

  • Start from corners and edges where clues are easier.
  • If a number already has enough flags around it, the rest of its neighbors are safe.
  • Not sure? Mark the cell and break through from another area first.
