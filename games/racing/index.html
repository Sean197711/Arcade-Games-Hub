🏎️ Dodge Racer

Drive down a four-lane highway, switch lanes to dodge traffic, and score for every car you pass.

Score0

🎯 How to play

  • Desktop: ←→ or AD to change lanes, Space to pause.
  • Mobile: tap the ◀ ▶ buttons below the game.
  • Avoid all oncoming cars; a crash ends the game.
  • Each car dodged scores 1 point; the speed keeps rising.

💡 Pro tips

  • Watch the traffic ahead and pick gaps early.
  • Don't swerve repeatedly — keep room to react.
  • Stay calm as the speed climbs; avoid collisions first.
