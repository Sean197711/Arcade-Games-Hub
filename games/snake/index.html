🐍 Snake

The arcade classic: eat food to grow longer — hit a wall or your own tail and it's over. The higher your score, the faster it gets!

Score0
Best0

🎯 How to play

  • Desktop: steer with ↑↓←→ or WASD, Space to pause/resume.
  • Mobile: tap the arrow buttons below the screen.
  • Each food is worth 10 points and grows the snake by one segment.
  • Hitting a wall or your own body ends the game.
  • The snake speeds up as your score grows; your best score is saved on this device.

💡 Pro tips

  • Stay near the edges to keep room to maneuver.
  • Once the snake grows long, avoid dead ends.
  • Plan your turns early — don't swerve right at the food.
