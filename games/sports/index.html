⚽ Hoops Shoot

Drag on the screen to set angle and power, release to shoot. Score every ball that drops through the hoop.

Score0
Shots0

🎯 How to play

  • Press and drag on the screen, then release to shoot toward the hoop.
  • Or click Start for an auto-aimed shot.
  • A ball through the hoop scores; misses can be retried.
  • The more you sink in a row, the higher your score.

💡 Pro tips

  • Aim so the trajectory points at the hoop center.
  • Use moderate power — too much or too little misses.
  • Sink consecutively to keep your rhythm.
