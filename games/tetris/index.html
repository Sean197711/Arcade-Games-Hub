🧱 Tetris

Rotate, move and stack the falling pieces — complete a row to clear it. Level up every 10 lines!

🎯 How to play

  • ←→ move the piece, ↓ speeds up the fall.
  • ↑ or W rotates, Space drops the piece instantly.
  • Complete a full row to clear it; clearing multiple rows at once scores more.
  • The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

💡 Pro tips

  • Keep one column open as a well for the I-piece to clear four lines.
  • Keep the surface flat and avoid holes.
  • At higher levels, plan where the next piece goes in advance.
