🧱 Tetris

Rotate and stack blocks; fill a full row to clear it. Level up every 10 lines — faster!

🎯 How to play

  • ←→ move, ↓ soft drop.
  • ↑/W rotate, Space hard drop.
  • Clear a full row to score; multiple rows at once score more.
  • Stack reaching the top ends the game.

💡 Pro tips

  • Leave a vertical well for the I-piece to clear four lines.
  • Keep the surface flat to avoid holes.
  • Plan the next piece's spot as levels rise.
